## Depth

- Strongest connected depth is in Bắc Nam Hà, Cái Lớn–Cái Bé, and Sông Hương.
- Nam Măng Thít and Ô Môn–Xà No have long quantitative water-quality reports, but facility-level operational decisions and longitudinal QA/QC remain limited.
- Many temporal bulletins are repeated products; they increase temporal resolution more than use-case diversity.

## Connectivity

- Three candidate packs were rechecked; **6** relations are source-supported, while applicability/comparability relations remain explicitly unverified or contradicted.
- Same basin/system is not treated as proof of rule applicability.

## Temporal coverage

- Sources range from historical/reference material through 2026 bulletins. Comparisons are valid only when entity, metric, unit, method, and aggregation window align.
- The Sông Hương event report contains an unresolved conflict between a November event heading and dates inside the narrative.

## Operational relevance

- Supported: abstraction schedules, seasonal water-supply risk, water-quality/salinity monitoring, reservoir/flood-event facts, document lineage, and safety/inspection requirements.
- Weak: canal-level allocation versus delivery, crop-stage scheduling, facility maintenance outcomes, dam instrumentation/inspection results, and emergency-response performance.

## Evidence usability and reliability

- Direct-text PDFs are generally source-locatable. Scanned decisions use OCR for discovery only; Decision 1606, 740 and 922 page 1 identities were checked against page images.
- OCR numeric values are not automatically promoted to verified facts.
- Draft/blank-header documents remain draft or legally unresolved; no human verification is claimed.
