# Scientific Before/After Comparison

## Corpus

- Before: the V4.4 inventory had 93 canonical records but many content claims remained `SOURCE_PRESENT_NOT_INSPECTED` or category-derived.
- After: **93/93** sources have an explicit page-bound screening locator, **20** sources have deeper page inspection, and **60** reusable evidence records carry PDF hashes and page locators.
- Improvement is evidence traceability, not an increase in PDF count. Remaining screening-only documents: **73**.

## Linked packs

- Before: three candidate packs and machine-proposed relations.
- After: **9** relations were re-audited; **6** are source-supported. Same-entity links are separated from unverified temporal/metric/applicability links.
- Genuine opportunities are bounded version reasoning, identity intersection, and compatibility/insufficiency analysis—not automatic rule application.

## QA

- Before: 38 user-retained pilot QA plus 10 V4.4 drafts.
- After: **48/48** remain in the inventory, **38/38** USER_KEEP decisions are preserved, and numeric/legal/temporal/competency risks are separated from retention status.
- Added: **8** manually curated drafts spanning **8** operational use cases and **7** source documents.
- Human-verified gold coverage remains **0**; all new QA remain pending human review.

## Interpretation

The principal gain is stronger source binding and broader operational use cases. It is not scientific progress to treat document/QA totals or test PASS counts as semantic gold.
